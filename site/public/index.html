Where should load balancing live?

A datacenter fabric offers many equal-cost paths between any two racks, and ECMP spreads traffic over them by hashing, without ever looking at congestion. CONGA (SIGCOMM 2014) argues that the fabric itself should sense congestion along whole paths and steer traffic around it; here you take that argument apart.

Work through three short puzzles, reading the paper passages in the margin as you go, then answer three questions. Your answers are saved in this browser as you work, and one Submit at the end sends everything.
