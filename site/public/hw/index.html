Where should load balancing live?

Unit 4 ended on a gap: the WCMP controller knows the topology but not the load right now, a switch sees only its own links, and a FlowBender sender gets feedback without knowing the state of the other paths. CONGA (SIGCOMM 2014) gives its own answer to who measures, who decides and who acts; here you follow how the paper gets there and test it on small fabrics where the paths are not equal.
